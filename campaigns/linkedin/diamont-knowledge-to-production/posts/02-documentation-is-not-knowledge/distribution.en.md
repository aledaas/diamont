# Distribution copy — Piece 02

Most teams do not have too little documentation.

They have too many places to look for it.

Tickets, ADRs, diagrams, messages, recordings, and prompts contain information. But finding a fragment does not prove that it is sufficient, current, or applicable to the problem in front of us.

Documentation remains essential. The mistake is confusing the container with the knowledge we intended to preserve.

In the second edition of **Knowledge → Production**, I explore a practical test:

> Can another person—or an agent—use our documentation to make the correct decision without manually reconstructing the original reasoning?

Where does your team's real knowledge live today?
