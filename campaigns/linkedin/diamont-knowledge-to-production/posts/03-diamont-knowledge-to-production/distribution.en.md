# Distribution copy — Piece 03

One person can now coordinate the productive capacity of five or more developers.

But not by asking five AI agents to write code at the same time.

Without shared understanding, parallel execution only creates parallel mistakes. Each worker reconstructs the missing context, makes locally reasonable decisions, and moves the system in a different direction—faster.

I created **DIAMONT** to solve that production problem.

It is a methodology for transforming human intent and experience into reusable knowledge, then using that knowledge to govern the work of people and AI workers with explicit authority and traceable results.

The person remains the authority.

The workers expand execution capacity.

The methodology keeps both connected.

In the third edition of **Knowledge → Production**, I explain the complete path from intent to observable production—and why access to more agents will not be the real competitive advantage.
