# Communication strategy

## Commercial objective

Position Alejandro Daas as the creator of DIAMONT and convert interest in the methodology into qualified conversations about his services in architecture, fintech platforms and AI-assisted engineering production.

Alejandro controls publication timing. The campaign owns message sequence, evidence, assets, navigation and learning from every delivery; it does not prescribe dates or weekdays.

## Central thesis

> Alejandro Daas created DIAMONT, an engineering production methodology that enables one responsible professional to coordinate multiple AI workers without losing business intent, reusable knowledge, human authority or traceability.

DIAMONT is provider-independent by design. Codex may be one worker environment, but the methodology must remain applicable to other agents and models through replaceable, capability-specific representations.

## Claim discipline

### Supported now

- DIAMONT was created by Alejandro Daas.
- It transforms intent and experience into reusable knowledge for production.
- It separates knowledge authority from agents and tools.
- It is designed for multiple replaceable human and artificial actors.
- Portfolio, Mission Control, MINA and Runner are its first operational ecosystem.
- It preserves approvals, execution context, evidence and learning.

### Demonstrated direction, requiring concrete examples

- One professional can govern several workers and workstreams through the ecosystem.
- Reusable knowledge can reduce repeated discovery and context reconstruction.
- Governed agent execution can increase an individual's effective production capacity.

### Hypothesis requiring measurement before publication as fact

> One person using DIAMONT produces the workforce capacity of five developers.

Until a reproducible case measures scope, elapsed time, human intervention, quality, rework and outcomes against a credible baseline, public material must describe this as the ambition or observed direction of DIAMONT—not as a proven `5x` result.

Acceptable language:

> DIAMONT is designed to let one professional direct a coordinated workforce of AI workers with the context and control normally missing from agentic development.

> In our current production, a single architect can coordinate multiple specialized workers while retaining approval authority and traceable outcomes.

Avoid for now:

> One person replaces five developers.

> DIAMONT makes every developer five times more productive.

## Required structure of every delivery

1. Open with a costly, recognizable production problem.
2. State Alejandro's point of view in plain language.
3. Introduce only the DIAMONT mechanism needed to resolve that problem.
4. Show evidence from the real ecosystem whenever available.
5. Name the limitation or maturity level honestly.
6. Connect the idea to a service Alejandro can provide.
7. End with one explicit next action: read, inspect, or start a conversation.

## Validation gate

Before a delivery is ready, verify:

- it clearly attributes DIAMONT to Alejandro;
- it explains value before internal vocabulary;
- it is understandable without reading the repository;
- it distinguishes specification, experiment and production evidence;
- it does not imply universal AI compatibility as proven when it is currently a design constraint;
- it does not publish an unmeasured productivity multiplier;
- it connects naturally to a service rather than adding a generic CTA at the end;
- every technical assertion has an identified source;
- its visual asset follows the LinkedIn safe-area contract;
- its navigation and UTM destination are complete.

## Evidence needed for the capacity claim

A future measured case should record:

- one bounded feature or production objective;
- the role and number of workers involved;
- work executed sequentially and in parallel;
- Alejandro's review, correction and approval time;
- elapsed production time;
- accepted output and rejected/reworked output;
- tests and observable outcome;
- comparison against a declared human-only or previous-process baseline;
- limits: complexity, context quality, tool capability and supervision required.

Only then should the campaign decide whether `5x`, another multiplier or a qualitative capacity claim is defensible.
