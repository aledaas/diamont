# LinkedIn About — English primary version

I am a Platform and Software Architect with more than 25 years of experience designing and building scalable digital systems.

My work sits at the intersection of software architecture, fintech platforms, technical leadership, and AI-assisted engineering. I have designed products and infrastructure for digital onboarding, wallets, payments, settlement, financial integrations, blockchain networks, and complex operational platforms.

Today I am building DIAMONT: an engineering production model that turns intent and experience into reusable knowledge, then connects that knowledge with governed, traceable software production.

Its first operational ecosystem includes:

• MINA — discovers applicable knowledge and exposes what is missing.
• Crystals — preserve engineering knowledge with identity, provenance, and purpose.
• Portfolio and Mission Control — govern requirements, decisions, approvals, execution, and evidence.
• Portfolio Runner — connects approved work with local tools and workers.

The goal is not simply to generate more code with AI. It is to create the conditions for people and agents to produce the right software without losing business intent, architectural reasoning, authority, or traceability.

I work with founders, CTOs, and engineering teams that are:

• building or modernizing fintech platforms;
• designing wallets, payment infrastructure, operational consoles, and financial APIs;
• introducing AI-assisted engineering responsibly;
• transforming fragmented technical knowledge into repeatable production capability;
• evolving critical systems without losing control of their architecture.

My core stack and experience include Laravel/PHP, Kotlin and mobile platforms, Vue/JavaScript, API and domain architecture, PostgreSQL, Stellar and multichain systems, product design, and technical leadership.

I write about this work in Knowledge → Production, my LinkedIn newsletter on architecture, reusable knowledge, fintech platforms, and AI-assisted software production.
