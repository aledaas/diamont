# Mission Control

## Purpose

Provide visibility and governance for production.

## Definition

Mission Control is the Portfolio interface that presents project context, production state, proposals, decisions, approvals, and operational evidence without defining DIAMONT or owning the Patrimony.

## Responsibilities

- Present MINA proposals and production state.
- Make decisions, approvals, and evidence visible.
- Preserve the separation between recommendation, approval, and execution.

## Relationships

- Presents proposals produced by [MINA](MINA.md).
- Makes [Requirements](REQUIREMENT.md) and production visible.
- Authorizes dispatch through the [Runner](RUNNER.md).
- Observes results returned by [Workers](WORKER.md).

## Used By

- Portfolio.
- Orfebres.
