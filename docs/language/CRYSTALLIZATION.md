# Crystallization

## Purpose

Make shared understanding durable without expanding or diverting its originating intention.

## Definition

Crystallization is the process by which contextual shared understanding is expressed as durable knowledge without hiding provenance, decisions, limits, or relevant uncertainties and without adding new objectives.

## Responsibilities

- Preserve the purity of the originating intention.
- Remove ambiguity after shared understanding exists.
- Preserve provenance, decisions, limits, and uncertainty.
- Keep production results separate from accepted knowledge until approval.
