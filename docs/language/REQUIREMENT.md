# Requirement

## Purpose

Express the change or effect that initiates production.

## Definition

A Requirement is the current engineering representation of an intention: a situated expression of a desired change or effect that may be incomplete, contradictory, or evolve during joint reasoning.

## Responsibilities

- Preserve the originating intention.
- Provide context and constraints for discovery.
- Precede tasks, solutions, and implementation.
