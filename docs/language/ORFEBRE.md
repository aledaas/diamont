# Orfebre

## Purpose

Contribute human engineering capability to DIAMONT production.

## Definition

An Orfebre is the human engineering role that participates in joint reasoning, decisions, approvals, production, and observation according to its capabilities and authority.

## Responsibilities

- Contribute human engineering judgment.
- Participate in shared understanding.
- Exercise only the authority assigned to the role.
- Review production and its evidence.

## Relationships

- Works within a [Software Factory](SOFTWARE_FACTORY.md).
- Interacts with [Mission Control](MISSION_CONTROL.md).
- Collaborates with [Workers](WORKER.md).
- Participates in [Crystallization](CRYSTALLIZATION.md).

## Used By

- Software Factory.
- Portfolio.
- Mission Control.
