# Face

## Purpose

Allow a Crystal to satisfy a particular need while preserving its identity.

## Definition

A Face is a virtue or dimension of knowledge through which a Crystal satisfies a particular need; it belongs to the same Crystal only while remaining coherent with that Crystal's conceptual identity and is never a version.

## Responsibilities

- Express a coherent virtue or dimension of a Crystal.
- Preserve the Crystal's permanent identity.
- Retain the evidence and genealogy of its incorporation.

## Relationships

- Belongs to one [Crystal](CRYSTAL.md).
- Is discovered and evaluated by [MINA](MINA.md).
- Is incorporated through controlled [Crystallization](CRYSTALLIZATION.md).
