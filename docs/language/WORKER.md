# Worker

## Purpose

Execute approved engineering operations and return their results.

## Definition

A Worker is an artificial engineering role that executes concrete operations on a project under defined authority and returns results without selecting Crystals, approving its own actions, or crystallizing knowledge.

## Responsibilities

- Execute only approved operations.
- Respect scope, constraints, and source authority.
- Return results, evidence, observations, and uncertainties.
- Keep facts, interpretations, hypotheses, and recommendations distinct.

## Relationships

- Receives dispatched actions from the [Runner](RUNNER.md).
- Collaborates with an [Orfebre](ORFEBRE.md) according to capability.
- Applies representations derived from [Crystals](CRYSTAL.md).
- Returns evidence for controlled [Crystallization](CRYSTALLIZATION.md).

## Used By

- Portfolio.
- Runner.
- DIAMONT research protocols.
- DIAMONT consumer projects.
