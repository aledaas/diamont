# MINA

## Purpose

Help resolve Requirements through the knowledge preserved by DIAMONT.

## Definition

MINA is the cognitive capability that safeguards, explores, and operates the Patrimony to help resolve Requirements without owning the knowledge, defining DIAMONT, administering projects, approving actions, or executing production.

## Responsibilities

- Discover Crystals and Faces.
- Evaluate sufficiency and detect knowledge gaps.
- Suggest traceable compositions and possible actions.
- Receive evidence and observations from production.

## Relationships

- Interprets [Requirements](REQUIREMENT.md) as assistance.
- Discovers [Crystals](CRYSTAL.md) and [Faces](FACE.md).
- Returns proposals to [Mission Control](MISSION_CONTROL.md).
- May be hosted locally by the [Runner](RUNNER.md) without belonging to it.

## Used By

- Portfolio.
- Runner.
- Workers.
- DIAMONT research protocols.
