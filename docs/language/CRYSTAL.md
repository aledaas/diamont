# Crystal

## Purpose

Preserve reusable engineering knowledge with permanent identity and provenance.

## Definition

A Crystal is a permanent unit of knowledge that emerges when shared understanding is crystallized; it has one identity, is never versioned or replaced, can gain Faces through recrystallization, and can relate to other Crystals.

## Responsibilities

- Preserve crystallized knowledge and its intention.
- Maintain permanent identity and genealogy.
- Relate knowledge without deriving identity from physical location.
- Evolve through Faces without versions.

## Relationships

- Emerges through [Crystallization](CRYSTALLIZATION.md).
- Gains capacity through [Faces](FACE.md).
- Is discovered and composed by [MINA](MINA.md).
- Guides [Workers](WORKER.md) through derived representations.
